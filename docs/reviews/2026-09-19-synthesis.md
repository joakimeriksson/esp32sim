# Review findings and dispositions — September 19, 2026

**The recorded correctness gates passed.** They cover the release workspace, firmware golden outputs, WASM firmware runs, JIT differential cases and browser-host checks. The [validation receipt](../evidence/review-2026-09-19/validation.json) records the exact scope and source differences, summarized below. Hardware-model gaps remain explicit and Pocket Tank performance work is still pending.

The reviews identified real input-validation, DMA, scheduling and TCP defects. The changes below fix those failures and consolidate duplicated rules without replacing the direct chip models with a framework. The most substantial corrections are covered by focused [parser](../../esp-soc/tests/parsers.rs), [DMA](../../esp32s3/src/bus/dma_tests.rs), [scheduler](../../esp32s3/tests/machine.rs) and [TCP](../../esp32s3/src/nat/tests.rs) regressions.

This report combines the attached numbered bug report, the attached structural review and `.pi/plans/2026-09-19-thermonuclear-review/review.md`. Bug numbers refer to the first attachment; P0/P1 labels refer to the second; T1–T16 and Tier 4 refer to the third. Those structural priority labels are not adopted as correctness severities. The comparison base is local merge `cfa669c4461e6c5b06f9740b2554686830c8d8de`, incorporating the [timing stack through PR #91](https://github.com/joakimeriksson/esp32sim/pull/91), [FlatRam bounds fix #92](https://github.com/joakimeriksson/esp32sim/pull/92) and [experiment catalog #93](https://github.com/joakimeriksson/esp32sim/pull/93). Current source and regression receipts are linked below. “Fixed” means the implementation and regression coverage are present; final integrated validation is reported separately at the end.

Several proposed remedies were wrong even where the underlying concern was sound: CUST slots contain real PIE instructions, the FFT load form intentionally omits its final result pair, SHA-224 uses SHA-256 compression and a 32-byte TLB layout assertion is wrong on wasm32. Those distinctions affect behavior and are explained in the corresponding rows. [ISA regressions](../../xtensa-lx7/tests/isa_regressions.rs), [SHA initialization](../../esp-periph/src/sha.rs), [TLB layout](../../emu-core/src/bus.rs)

**Structural review: accepted simplifications and rejected overreach**

The structural review's strongest findings concerned repeated policy, excessive module size and ambiguous boundaries. The fixes below assign those rules to one owner. Broad proposals to replace chip-specific buses, the ISA or every state field with generic machinery remain rejected where they would add contracts without eliminating the underlying differences. The current [bus](../../esp32s3/src/bus.rs), [JIT policy](../../xtensa-lx7/src/jit/wasm_policy.rs) and [browser dispatch](../../wasm/src/machine.rs) show the retained direct approach.

**Module size and remaining limits**

The supplied line counts predate local comparison base `cfa669c4461e6c5b06f9740b2554686830c8d8de`. At that base, `bus.rs`, `machine.rs`, `wasm_emit.rs`, `wasm_tests.rs` and `wasm/src/lib.rs` had already grown beyond those counts. The decomposition leaves each of these files below 1,000 lines while placing the extracted logic in focused modules; this is a source-organization improvement, not a claim that all extracted lines disappeared. [bus](../../esp32s3/src/bus.rs), [Machine](../../esp-soc/src/machine.rs), [emitter](../../xtensa-lx7/src/jit/wasm_emit.rs), [oracle harness](../../xtensa-lx7/src/jit/wasm_tests.rs), [ABI](../../wasm/src/lib.rs)

The concrete remaining limitations include timer-group watchdogs, C6 LP watchdogs, RTC watchdog interrupt routing and undocumented FFT combinations. The precise coprocessor-enable requirement for the two floating-register PIE transfers remains unresolved and needs a silicon probe. NAT still exposes host-reachable destinations when enabled. The explicitly retained structural opportunities include frontend rendering/status duplication, small register/name-table duplication and bus event/GPIO coordination. Those are disclosed above rather than counted as completed fixes. [watchdog coverage](../peripherals.md), [C6 watchdog coverage](../esp32c6.md), [FFT and current coprocessor gate](../../xtensa-lx7/src/pie.rs), [floating-transfer test scope](../../xtensa-lx7/tests/isa_regressions.rs), [NAT policy](../cli.md), [UI](../../web/run.html), [bus coordination](../../esp32s3/src/bus.rs)
